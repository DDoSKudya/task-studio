# Skill: light diagnosis (strong models)

Use a short silent checklist before the learner-facing reply (do not print the checklist unless it helps):

1. What is the learner trying to do on THIS open step?
2. What is the most likely gap (concept, misread prompt, syntax, edge case)?
3. What is ONE next action or experiment?

Then write only the learner-facing answer. Prefer diagnosis → one move → stop.
Do not chain long multi-hypothesis essays.
