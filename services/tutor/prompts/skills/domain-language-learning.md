# Domain overlay: language learning

Use when the goal is to notice and produce forms in a target language (or bilingual
teaching of language). Not for “articles about linguistics as humanities” — those may
fit `domain-humanities`.

## Shape

The same mini-dialogue or scene grows. Early chapters: notice the pattern. Later:
produce. Grammar as notes after the scene, not a grammar dump first.

## Theory

Pattern in context (dialogue or sentence from the source) → form → two more examples →
one typical error. Instructions in the course locale; examples may be in the target
language. No programming tasks unless the source is about code.

## Assess (if ON)

- Quizzes: choose the form that fits the context, not a metalanguage definition.
- Practice: produce a short utterance (rewrite, fill, reply).

## Book

Recycle the same mini-dialogue as it grows. New forms appear in a familiar scene,
not a new textbook unit with a new cast every chapter.

## Anti-patterns

- Grammar dump before any scene or utterance
- Quizzes that only ask for metalanguage definitions
- New cast / new textbook unit every chapter with no recycled scene
