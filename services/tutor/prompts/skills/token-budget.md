# Skill: token budget (weak / local models)

Context and tokens are expensive on small local models / CPU Ollama. Optimize for a correct short reply.

- Typical reply: ≤ 80 words unless the learner asks for more depth.
- At most one short code/SQL fence (≤ 4 lines).
- Do not restate the full outline or paste large page excerpts back.
- Do not bilingual-gloss the same idea (no "names / имена / فقط …").
- Prefer bullets over paragraphs.
- If unsure, one clarifying question beats a speculative essay.
- Do not enable long “thinking” dumps — tutoring needs a usable answer, not a chain-of-thought transcript.
